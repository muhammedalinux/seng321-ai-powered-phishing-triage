## 4. Potential Blockers

| Potential Blocker | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Delayed SRS or NFR documentation | Delays traceability and architecture reviews. | Coordinate with the responsible team member and track progress. |
| Missing Figma prototype links | Prevents complete UI/UX traceability. | Request accessible links from the UI/UX owner. |
| Inconsistent requirement IDs | Causes incorrect mappings between documents. | Verify IDs across SRS, backlog, and quality specifications. |
| Incomplete architecture diagrams | Delays technical review and RTM completion. | Coordinate with the Software Architect. |
| Unreviewed Pull Requests | May delay final submission. | Request timely reviews and track outstanding PRs. |

These are potential risks, not confirmed incidents. Actual blockers and resolutions will be recorded in the Sprint 2 progress report.
